Selected research

Adaptive Visual Input for Real-time Video MLLMs

Mar 2026 — Present

Studying how frame rate, resolution, and scene dynamics affect motion understanding and model latency in real-time multimodal systems.

2,000+ MotionBench QA samples
2 models Qwen2.5-VL-7B · InternVL3-8B
Full pipeline Sampling · inference · analysis
  • Built RTV-Eval, a reproducible evaluation framework with video sampling, MLLM inference, answer parsing, SQLite checkpointing, and batched model-by-strategy experiments.
  • Designed an adaptive upload strategy using low-resolution frame difference and SSIM to adjust frame rate, resolution, and keyframes to scene dynamics.
  • In a preliminary 50-question StreamingBench evaluation, improved accuracy from 66% to 74% while reducing model TTFT from 335.8 ms to 286.9 ms against a fixed low-frame-rate baseline.
